Defense Knowledge

How To Identify Social Engineering Attacks

Over 90% of cyber breaches begin with an unsolicited message or deceptive SMS.

🔗 URL Deception

The Shortened Link Trap

Scammers use URL shorteners like bit.ly or tinyurl to hide the true destination. Never click shortened links from unknown numbers.

⏳ Psychological Pressure

Artificial Time Limits

Phrases like "Account suspended in 24 hours" are engineered to make you panic. Legitimate services do not freeze accounts without warning.

🔑 Secret Credentials

The One-Time Code Rule

Your OTP (One-Time Password) is exclusively for you. No customer support agent, police officer, or bank fraud team will ever request it.

🛡️ Active Defense

Forward SMS to 7726

In the US, UK, and Canada, forward scam SMS messages to 7726 (spells SPAM). Your wireless carrier investigates and blocks the offending source.
