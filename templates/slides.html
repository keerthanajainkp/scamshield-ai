Problem Statement

The Explosive Rise of Social Engineering Scams

💥 $10+ Billion Lost

Consumers lose billions annually to fraudulent SMS and deceptive email phishing. SMS has a 98% open rate, making smishing 3x more dangerous than email fraud.

⏳ Panic Manipulation

Attackers weaponize artificial urgency ("Account suspended within 24 hours!") to force victims into clicking before verifying with their bank.

🔒 Privacy Barrier

Most existing AI detectors require uploading personal messages to third-party cloud APIs. Users cannot safely upload confidential texts without risking their privacy.

Speaker Note: "Judges, the problem is massive. Over 10 billion dollars is lost annually. Scammers exploit fear and urgency, and users have had no private, local way to check messages safely."
The Solution

ScamShield AI: Your Personal Threat Scanner

⚡ Instant Threat Index

Animated circular gauge provides an intuitive 0% to 100% score with clear classifications: Verified Clean (Green), Elevated Risk (Amber), or Critical Risk (Red).

🛡️ 4-Pillar Security Diagnostic

Deconstructs every message across Link Integrity, Urgency Pressure, Financial Bait, and Credential Requests with exact matched snippets.

🔐 100% Privacy-Preserving

Zero external API calls. Zero message storage. All detection happens locally on the user's laptop in less than 1 millisecond.

📋 Actionable Defense Checklist

Provides empowering next steps—such as forwarding SMS to 7726 to alert wireless carriers—plus 1-click report copying.

Speaker Note: "ScamShield AI provides immediate, explainable threat intelligence. It diagnoses messages in under 1ms, shows why a message was flagged, and provides immediate safety steps."
Detection Logic

The 4 Core Security Pillars

🔗 Link Integrity

  • Shortened URLs (`bit.ly`, `tinyurl`)
  • Direct IP addresses (`192.168.1.1`)
  • High-risk TLDs (`.xyz`, `.top`)
  • Deceptive login keywords

⏳ Pressure & Urgency

  • Coercive time limits ('24 hours')
  • Account freeze threats
  • Fake police/arrest threats
  • Panic-inducing phrasing

🎁 Prize & Bait

  • Unearned lotteries ($1,000,000)
  • Crypto multiplier giveaways
  • Fake retail gift cards
  • Unclaimed inheritance claims

🔑 Credential Theft

  • Password & PIN demands
  • One-Time Passcodes (OTP)
  • Social Security Numbers (SSN)
  • Credit card CVV codes
Speaker Note: "Our engine breaks threats into four pillars: Link Integrity, Urgency, Prize Bait, and Credential Theft. These four vectors cover the vast majority of consumer fraud."
